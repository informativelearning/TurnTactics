Prototype rule assumptions
  • Default board: (players − 1) × (players − 1), configurable at setup.
  • Moves cost 1 point per orthogonal tile; occupied tiles are blocked.
  • Range uses Manhattan distance and applies to attacks and gifts. Range upgrades persist.
  • Blocked actions cost nothing. Giving a point transfers exactly 1, with no additional fee.
  • Votes require more than half of votes cast; abstentions excluded. Ballots reset daily.
  • Rule changes and custom-image player icons are not implemented yet.
