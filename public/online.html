    THE GROUND RULES

    Small rules. Real consequences.

    Move · 1 point
    One empty orthogonal square. No diagonals.
    Attack / give · 1 point
    Within your Manhattan range. An unshielded attack removes one heart; giving transfers one point.
    Shield · 2 points
    Protects until midnight; buying it again while shielded is unavailable.
    Range · 2 points
    Increases your range by one for the rest of this game.
    Pacific time
    Normal games open at the next midnight. Daily income arrives at midnight. Actions close at 7 pm. Unspent points accumulate.
    Eliminated players
    Can vote for a living player. A strict majority of ballots cast awards one point at midnight. No majority means no bonus.
    Referee visibility
    The host sees every resource and a private action log. Other players do not.
