      THE GROUND RULES

      Small rules. Real consequences.

      Move · 1 point
      One empty orthogonal square. No diagonals. Occupied squares block movement.
      Attack / give · 1 point
      Starting Manhattan range: 3. Within range, an unshielded attack removes one heart; giving transfers one point.
      Shield · 2 points
      Protects until midnight; buying it again while shielded is unavailable.
      Range · 2 points
      Increases your range by one for the rest of this game.
      Pacific time
      Normal games open at the next midnight. Daily income arrives at midnight. Actions close at 6 pm. Unspent points accumulate.
      Eliminated players
      All remaining points are lost on elimination. Can vote for a living player. A strict majority of ballots cast awards one point at midnight. No majority means no bonus.
      Add Change · 5 points
      Host approval required. Payment is on approval; rejected proposals cost nothing. In slideshow rooms, approved changes and their proposers are public. Legacy rooms keep their previous publication rules.
      Slideshow board schedule
      5 players: 8 × 8; 7: 9 × 9; 8–9: 10 × 10; 10: 11 × 11; 11–12: 12 × 12; 13–14: 13 × 13; 15–16: 14 × 14; 17–20: 15 × 15. Six players are not specified. Legacy rooms retain their original board.
      Explicit declarations
      Use the confirmation panel to declare your action. Only a server-accepted action changes the game. A rejected or interrupted request is not an undeclared action and does not forfeit points.
      Referee visibility
      The host sees every resource and a private action log, but must not reveal private stats or actions. Players may choose to reveal their own information.
